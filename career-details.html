Job Overview

We are looking for a talented and passionate AI Engineer to join our team. As an AI Engineer, you will design, develop, and deploy machine learning models and AI-driven solutions that power our core products. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams including data scientists, backend developers, and product managers to build scalable, intelligent systems that solve real-world challenges.

If you are driven by a passion for artificial intelligence, a problem-solving mindset, and the ability to turn complex data into impactful solutions, we want you to join our growing team!

Key Responsibilities

  • Model Development: Design, train, and optimize machine learning and deep learning models to solve complex business problems.
  • Data Pipeline Engineering: Build and maintain robust data pipelines for ingesting, processing, and transforming large-scale datasets for model training and inference.
  • Model Deployment & Monitoring: Deploy AI models to production environments and continuously monitor performance, accuracy, and reliability.
  • Cross-Team Collaboration: Partner with product managers, backend engineers, and business stakeholders to translate requirements into AI-powered features.
  • Research & Innovation: Stay up to date with the latest AI/ML research and evaluate new tools, frameworks, and methodologies to continuously improve our systems.
  • Documentation & Reporting: Document model architectures, experiments, and outcomes clearly for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Qualifications & Skills

  • Experience: 3–5 years of hands-on experience in AI/ML engineering, including model development and production deployment.
  • Technical Proficiency: Strong command of Python and ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn.
  • Cloud & MLOps: Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and MLOps tools for model versioning, CI/CD, and scalable deployment.
  • Analytical Thinking: Strong mathematical foundation in statistics, linear algebra, and probability for model development and evaluation.
  • Communication: Ability to clearly communicate complex AI concepts to both technical teams and non-technical stakeholders.

Why Join Us?

  • Fully remote position with flexible working hours.
  • Work on cutting-edge AI projects alongside top-tier engineers and researchers.
  • Continuous learning budget for courses, conferences, and certifications.
  • Competitive salary of $15K–$20K annually with performance-based growth opportunities.
